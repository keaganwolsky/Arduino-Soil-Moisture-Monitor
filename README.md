This Arduino-based soil moisture monitor functions by measuring the electrical resistance between two probes.
As moisture levels in the soil increase, conductivity rises, causing the resistance to drop. Consequently, a higher analog reading indicates drier soil.
I have established a threshold of 800: when the value exceeds this limit, a red LED activates and the LCD displays "I need water!" Conversely, if the value remains below 800, a green LED turns on and the screen reads "I am happy." 
Additionally, a potentiometer is integrated into the circuit to calibrate the display's contrast for optimal visibility.
